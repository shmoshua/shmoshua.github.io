--- title: "Weak Poincaré for SBM" status: open summary: "Can the SBM posterior satisfy a weak Poincaré inequality with polynomial ρ and a small magnetization defect?" ---

Setting up the inequality

Let $\mu$ be the posterior. For weak recovery, we denote the magnetization as $m(\sigma):= \langle x^*,\sigma\rangle / n$ where $x^*$ is the truth. So, we want a weak Poincare inequality of the following form: $$ \E_\mu g^2 \le \rho \dirchform{P}{g}{g} + \eps \E_\mu [m^2 g^2] $$ for all even $g$. Goal is to find whether we can have $\rho =n^{O(1)}$ and small $\eps$. If this holds, then for any even law $\nu$ with $f:= d\nu / d\mu$, by taking $g:= \sqrt{f}$ we have: $$ 1 = \E_\nu[1] =\E_\mu[g^2] \le \frac{\rho}{4} \dirchform{P}{f}{\log f}+\eps \E_\nu[m^2] $$ Therefore, $$ \E_{\nu}[m^2]\ge \frac{1-\frac{\rho}{4} \dirchform{P}{f}{\log f}}{\eps} $$

This might be too strong. Is there is a single trap, this might fail?

Find the dual?

Let us define: $$ \kappa(u):=\inf_{\begin{subarray}{c} \E_\mu g^2 = 1 \\ \E_\mu m^2 g^2 \le u\end{subarray}} \dirchform{P}{g}{g} $$ If $\kappa(u)\ge \kappa_0> 0$, then for any $g$ with $\E_\mu g^2 =1$, we have $\kappa_0\le \dirchform{P}{g}{g}$ or $u\le \E_\mu m^2 g^2 $. Hence, $$ \E_\mu g^2 \le \frac{1}{\kappa_0}\dirchform{P}{g}{g} + \frac{1}{u} \E_\mu m^2 g^2 $$ This can be generalized to any $g$ by taking $g / \|g\|_\mu$.

Now the dual is given by, finding a positive $h$ s.t. $$ \frac{(I-P)h}{h}+\theta m^2 \ge \kappa $$, then we have that: $$ \E_\mu g^2 \le \frac{1}{\kappa}\dirchform{P}{g}{g} +\frac{\theta }{\kappa} \E_\mu m^2 g^2 $$

Restricting Function Space Further

We only need the weak Poincare to hold for $\nu_t$ where $t\sim \text{Unif}[0,T]$. This means we can have following constraints:
  1. $\nu$ is even.
  2. We have that: $$ 0\le f_T = f_0 -\int_0^T (I-P) f_\tau d\tau = f_0 -T(I-P) f_t $$
Hence, we can solve the optimization problem: $$ \begin{array}{rl} \inf& \E_\mu [m^2 f]\\ \text{s.t.}& f\ge 0\\ & \E_\pi f = 1 \\ & \Ent_\mu[f]\le \text{KL}(\nu_0\|\mu)\\ & \dirchform{P}{f}{\log f} \le \text{KL}(\nu_0\|\mu) / T\\ & (I-P) f \le f_0/T \end{array} $$
