Setup

Let $k\ge 2$ be a fixed natural number. We study the $k$-community SBM. We have a random variable $(\sigma, G)\sim \text{SBM}_k(n,a,b)$ where $\sigma \sim\text{Unif}([k]^n)$ and for every $i < j$ independently, $$ \P(ij\in E_G)= P_{\sigma_i\sigma_j},\qquad P=\frac{1}{n}\left[(a-b) I+b 11^\top \right] $$ This model is called assortative or ferromagnetic, given $a > b$, which we will focus on in this note.

There are two goals:

  1. Detection: Let $G\sim G(n,d/n)$ where $d:=\frac{a + (k-1) b}{k}$ be the average degree. Let us denote this model as the null model. Can we easily distinguish between SBM and the null model?
  2. Recovery: Reconstruct or approximate the labels $\sigma$.

Kesten-Stigum Threshold

Mossel-Neeman-Sly have found out that the Kesten-Stigum threshold is given by: $$ a- b > k \sqrt{d} $$ We will study the derivation of this:

Investigating the Loops

Let $P,Q$ denote the measure of $G$ given by SBM and the null model respectively. Then, one way to distinguish $P$ and $Q$ is to look at the loops, as they are indistinguishable on trees.

We first have that: $$ \begin{aligned} \E_P[\#\text{ loops of size }\ell] & \approx \frac{1}{2\ell}\sum_{\sigma_1,..,\sigma_\ell \in [k]}\frac{1}{k^\ell}\left(\prod_{i\in[\ell]} P_{\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}}\right) n^{\ell}\\ &= \frac{1}{2\ell}\sum_{\sigma_1,..,\sigma_\ell \in [k]}\prod_{i\in[\ell]} \frac{n}{k}P_{\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}}\\ &= \frac{1}{2\ell}\tr(\Phi^{\ell}) \end{aligned} $$ where $\Phi:=\frac{n}{k}P$. We have the following two eigenvalues:

  1. $\Phi 1 = \frac{1}{k}[(a-b) + kb ] 1= d1$
  2. For any $v$ with $\langle 1, v\rangle = 0$, $\Phi v = \frac{1}{k}[(a-b)v]= \frac{a-b}{k}v$
Therefore, $$ \E_P[\#\text{ loops of size }\ell] \approx \frac{1}{2\ell}\left[d^\ell + (k-1) \left(\frac{a-b}{k}\right)^\ell\right] $$

However, we have that in Erdös-Renyi graph, $\#\ell \sim \text{Poisson}(\frac{d^\ell}{2\ell})$. Hence, $$ \E_Q[\#\text{ loops of size }\ell] = \frac{d^\ell}{2\ell} $$

So we can tell $P$ from $Q$ from one sample, only when the SBM's extra mean $(k-1)((a-b)/k)^\ell$ is large compared with that spread. In other words, $$ \frac{k-1}{2\ell}\left(\frac{a-b}{k}\right)^\ell \ge \sqrt{\frac{d^\ell}{2\ell}} $$

and by taking $\ell$ to infinity, we get that $a-b \ge k \sqrt{d}$: the Kesten-Stigum threshold.

Belief Propagation POV

Assume we now have $M:= \frac{1}{kd}[(a-b)I + b11^\top]$. Then, $M$ is a stochastic matrix and we can write it as: $$ M= \lambda I + (1-\lambda)\frac{1}{k}11^\top $$ where $\lambda = (a-b) / kd$, as $1- \lambda = \frac{kd - a+b}{kd} = \frac{a+(k-1)b - a+ b}{kd} = \frac{b}{d}$. Then, from belief propagation recall that the messages are given as: $$ \begin{aligned} m_{i\to j}(s) &\propto \prod_{\ell\in\partial i \backslash j} \sum_t m_{\ell\to i}(t)\exp(-\beta h(s,t))\\ &\propto \prod_{\ell\in\partial i \backslash j} \sum_t m_{\ell\to i}(t)M_{st} \end{aligned} $$ Now, consider the case where $m_{\ell \to i}(t) = 1/k$ for all $\ell$. Then, $$ m_{i\to j}(s) = \frac{k^{1- d(i)}}{k ^{2 - d(i)}} = \frac{1}{k} $$ Therefore, $m\equiv 1/k$ is a fixed point. Let $P_{i\to j}(s) := \prod_{\ell\in\partial i \backslash j} \sum_t m_{\ell\to i}(t)M_{st}$. Here we have that the derivatives are given by: $$ \begin{aligned} \frac{\partial m_{i\to j}(s)}{\partial m_{\ell\to i}(t)} &=\frac{1}{Z_{i\to j}}\frac{\partial P_{i\to j}(s)}{\partial m_{\ell \to i}(t)} - \frac{P_{i\to j}(s)}{Z_{i\to j}^2}\frac{\partial Z_{i\to j}}{\partial m_{\ell\to i}(t)}\\ &=\frac{1}{k ^{2 - d(i)}}M_{st} \cdot k ^{2 - d(i)}-\frac{1}{k^{3-d(i)}}k ^{2 - d(i)}\\ &= M_{st} - \frac{1}{k} \end{aligned} $$ Therefore, we get that the Jacobian of all messages is given by: $$ \left(M-\frac{1}{k}11^\top\right)\otimes B,\qquad B_{(k\to i)(\ell \to j)}=\begin{cases}1 & i = \ell \land k \ne j\\0 & \text{else}\end{cases} $$ where $B$ is called the non-backtracking matrix. So using a spectral algorithm, we can differentiate the two distributions if: $$ \mu = \frac{a-b}{k} > \sqrt{d} $$ Now, given the uniform fixed point, it would be quite useless if the fixed point is stable and stayed there the whole time. Hence, we are interested when BP fixed point is unstable. This is exactly when the spectral radius $$\rho((M-\frac{1}{k}11^\top )\otimes B) > 1.$$ Notice that $\text{Spec}(M-\frac{1}{k}11^\top ) =\{\lambda, \cdots\}$ and $\text{Spec}(B) = \{d, \mu, ...\}$. However, $\lambda d$ is from the uniform vector and is uninformative. Hence, the desired conclusion comes from: $$ \lambda \mu = \frac{(a-b)^2}{k^2d} > 1 $$ which is exactly the KS-threshold.

Results

Kesten–Stigum threshold

Weak recovery is possible if and only if $(a-b)^2 > 2(a+b)$.

Equivalently, with $a = d(1+\varepsilon)$, $b = d(1-\varepsilon)$ and $\lambda = \varepsilon\sqrt d$: weak recovery is possible if and only if $\lambda > 1$.
