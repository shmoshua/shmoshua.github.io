Setup

Let $k\ge 2$ be a fixed natural number. We study the $k$-community SBM. We have a random variable $(\sigma, G)\sim \text{SBM}_k(n,a,b)$ where $\sigma \sim\text{Unif}([k]^n)$ and for every $i < j$ independently, $$ \P(ij\in E_G)= P_{\sigma_i\sigma_j},\qquad P=\frac{1}{n}\left[(a-b) I+b 11^\top \right] $$ This model is called assortative or ferromagnetic, given $a > b$, which we will focus on in this note.

There are two goals:

  1. Detection: Let $G\sim G(n,d/n)$ where $d:=\frac{a + (k-1) b}{k}$ be the average degree. Let us denote this model as the null model. Can we easily distinguish between SBM and the null model?
  2. Recovery: Reconstruct or approximate the labels $\sigma$.

Kesten-Stigum Threshold

Mossel-Neeman-Sly have found out that the Kesten-Stigum threshold is given by: $$ a- b > k \sqrt{d} $$ We will study the derivation of this:

Investigating the Loops

Let $P,Q$ denote the measure of $G$ given by SBM and the null model respectively. Then, one way to distinguish $P$ and $Q$ is to look at the loops, as they are indistinguishable on trees.

We first have that: $$ \begin{aligned} \E_P[\#\text{ loops of size }\ell] & \approx \frac{1}{2\ell}\sum_{\sigma_1,..,\sigma_\ell \in [k]}\frac{1}{k^\ell}\left(\prod_{i\in[\ell]} P_{\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}}\right) n^{\ell}\\ &= \frac{1}{2\ell}\sum_{\sigma_1,..,\sigma_\ell \in [k]}\prod_{i\in[\ell]} \frac{n}{k}P_{\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}}\\ &= \frac{1}{2\ell}\tr(\Phi^{\ell}) \end{aligned} $$ where $\Phi:=\frac{n}{k}P$. We have the following two eigenvalues:

  1. $\Phi 1 = \frac{1}{k}[(a-b) + kb ] 1= d1$
  2. For any $v$ with $\langle 1, v\rangle = 0$, $\Phi v = \frac{1}{k}[(a-b)v]= \frac{a-b}{k}v$
Therefore, $$ \E_P[\#\text{ loops of size }\ell] = \frac{1}{2\ell}\left[d^\ell + (k-1) \left(\frac{a-b}{k}\right)^\ell\right] $$

Results

Kesten–Stigum threshold

Weak recovery is possible if and only if $(a-b)^2 > 2(a+b)$.

Equivalently, with $a = d(1+\varepsilon)$, $b = d(1-\varepsilon)$ and $\lambda = \varepsilon\sqrt d$: weak recovery is possible if and only if $\lambda > 1$.

Mossel–Neeman–Sly (impossibility below); Massoulié and Mossel–Neeman–Sly (algorithms above).
