An improved weak Poincare inequality for $0\le \beta < 1$ with only polynomial dependence on the defect $\eps$, using this it turns the annealing Glauber framework to polytime sampler with inverse poly TV error.

Let $A$ be the SK-model interaction matrix.

The biggest results are two things. They first introduce the weak Poincare inequality and then using that to give a simulated annealing algortihm.

For the setting of this paper, we say $0\le \beta < 1$ is the fixed inverse temperature, $D>0$ the error accuracy and $M > 0$ how close we want to get to the Gibbs distribution.

Theorem (Weak Poincare Inequality)

With probability at least $1-n^{-D}$, for every function $f:\Omega \to \R$ and $n^{-M}\le \eps\le 1/2$, we have that: $$ \Var_{\mu_A}(f)\le \poly(n)\cdot \left(\frac{1}{\eps}\right)^{O(1)}\cdot \dirchform{\mu_A}{f}{f}+\eps \cdot \text{osc}(f)^2 $$
